# Studio assets

Poly Haven CC0 assets, downloaded at 1K resolution:

- oak_veneer_01: desk and wall surfaces
- marble_01: desaturated stone floor surface
- lebombo: indoor HDR environment lighting
- potted_plant_02: plant and planter geometry
- sofa_03: legacy lounge asset, no longer loaded; the current lounge uses custom modular furniture

Source: https://polyhaven.com/ — https://polyhaven.com/license

The existing employee GLB models retain their original hosted URLs and provenance from the project. The original assets are static generated meshes. The application binds lightweight procedural rigs for ambient hand movement and walking, and places staff in desk and meeting scenes. These visual activities do not execute agent tasks.

Meshoptimizer 0.22 by Arseny Kapoulkine is distributed under the MIT license. The bundled module retains its license header. Three.js 0.160.1 is loaded from jsDelivr under the MIT license.
